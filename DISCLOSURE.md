# AI-use disclosure

Per the Into the Scrape-Verse rules:

**Claude Code** (and other LLMs) were used as pair-programmers throughout
the build. The architecture, product decisions and verification policy are
the participant's, who reviewed the code and performed the human review of
every collector's first output.

**Bright Data's Scraper Studio AI** generated the extraction code behind
every `c_*` collector and rewrote it during heals — that is the platform
being showcased.

The dashboard's visual design system is adapted from the participant's own
earlier work, reimplemented here for Mycelium's content and live data.
